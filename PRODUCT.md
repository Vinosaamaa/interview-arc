# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Interview Arc is an owner-private workspace for one candidate managing an
ongoing software-engineering interview journey. The owner plans practice,
records truthful attempt evidence, tracks real hiring processes, and returns to
durable knowledge without reconstructing prior context from memory.

## Product Purpose

Interview Arc joins interview practice, real company-and-role hiring Loops,
learning, engineering reflection, career materials, and longitudinal Journey
facts in one system. Success means the owner can see what is planned, what
actually happened, what remains unresolved, and which immutable context shaped
an activity without losing privacy or historical truth.

## Positioning

The product connects owner-authorized practice evidence to durable, revisioned
context. It preserves the distinction between plans and outcomes, exact and
reconstructed memory, reusable knowledge and dated attempts, and private source
material and display-safe projections.

## Operating Context

The web workspace coordinates three practice specialists, an administrative
Loop Recorder, a native Voice companion, Cloudflare Worker APIs, owner-isolated
D1 state, private R2 audio, and Git-owned published narrative artifacts. The
owner commonly uses Today while practicing, Loops around live hiring rounds,
and Journey for factual reflection across time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interview navigation owns Today, Loops, Reviews, Past, and Banks.
- The shared workspace shell has exactly three top-level workspaces: Interview,
  Learn, and Engineering. Interview owns its factual Journey; Learn and
  Engineering remain intentionally unavailable until their own bounded work
  begins, when each receives a separate local Statistics surface.
- One Loop represents one company-and-role process with flexible ordered stages
  and immutable Loop-owned Role Brief revisions.
- Only the Loop Recorder creates or revises Role Briefs. Practice specialists
  consume display-safe exact revisions and may attach context when authorized.
- D1 is authoritative for mutable owner-private state. Git is not a private
  database, and raw job descriptions, transcripts, recordings, or credentials
  never enter public fixtures or UI telemetry.
- Dates, outcomes, interviewer feedback, and remembered exactness are explicit
  facts; the product never infers them from elapsed or scheduled time.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Interview Arc. The incumbent interface uses a calm,
high-contrast operating workspace with deep green, white and pale botanical
surfaces, lime signal color, restrained specialty accents, Geist workhorse
type, and selective editorial serif display. The approved Loops handoff mockup
is binding composition evidence for this surface.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains authoritative question banks, practice history,
revisioned profiles, runtime contracts, and D1 projections. Loops may initially
be empty and must render a truthful empty state. Demonstration companies,
outcomes, dates, or interview facts must not be fabricated in production.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve truth before optimizing convenience.
2. Keep private sources behind display-safe projections.
3. Use stable identity and immutable revisions for durable context.
4. Make current work obvious without erasing historical evidence.
5. Separate administrative recording from specialist coaching.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must remain keyboard operable, screen-reader labelled,
responsive on desktop and mobile web, usable with reduced motion, and legible
at WCAG AA contrast.
