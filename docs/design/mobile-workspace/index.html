The design direction
  1. One compact header. Logo, workspace switch, and tools stay together.
  2. Content starts immediately. Short titles replace the oversized mobile banners.
  3. A list, not a stack of panels. Single-line titles, a quiet metadata line, and one action. Tap to read the full title.
  4. One reading surface. Full-width text, 17 px body copy, and code with Wrap and Copy controls.
  5. Predictable navigation. Sheets dismiss with Close or a tap outside. Returning from a reader restores your place.

Grounded in the existing product

Interview Arc’s teal, mineral paper, specialty colors, editorial titles, and original mark. The phone structure changes; desktop is outside this proposal.
